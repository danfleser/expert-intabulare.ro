## 1. Solution at a glance

- **The ranking problem is solved; the routing problem is not.** Two days after launch the site holds #1, #2, #2, #4, #5 on real commercial Alba queries. On almost every one of them **Google shows the wrong page** — a 1,000-person village instead of the city, or the house page instead of the apartment page.
- **Nothing in the original plan addresses this.** Rewriting a page Google never shows changes nothing. Routing comes first, content second.
- **Build the page the county queries actually deserve:** a service×county hub (`/servicii/intabulare-teren/alba/`). Today no such page exists, so Google picks a random village leaf. 12 new pages for Alba, one generator change.
- **Rebuild the meta description on 1,508 pages.** Today every one of them advertises *"aprox. 55 km de Aiud"* — the distance from the customer — in the Google snippet, in a near-identical string. Meta tags sit outside the quality gate, so this is a free, zero-risk change.
- **Make the link structure a hierarchy instead of a flat mesh.** Every village page currently gets about as much internal linking as Alba Iulia. Towns must outrank villages by construction.
- **Six flagship pages, chosen by measured rank, not by guesswork** — 4 Alba Iulia + 2 Aiud, every one of them a query where we already rank and Google serves a sibling.
- **Add one small generator slot (`serviceNotes`)** so the apartment page and the land page in the same town can finally say different things. This is the direct cure for the second failure mode.
- **Conversion work stays, trimmed:** the "what to send on WhatsApp" block, the named-surveyor block, the register sweep. All verified gate-safe.
- **The cheapest lever on the whole board is not code: Google reviews.** We are #1 in the Aiud map pack with **3** reviews. Ocna Mureș is held by firms with 1 and 4. Alba Iulia leaders have 32–39.
- **Everything is a hypothesis until 2026-08-19.** SERP data is correlational. The measurement gate decides what gets promoted to all 2,095 pages; nothing gets promoted on the strength of this analysis alone.

## 2. USER FLOWS — what a customer actually experiences

Each flow shows **today** (what really happens, from the captured Google results) and **after** (what the work above is meant to produce). The "after" is the intent, not a promise — see §5.

---

### Flow 1 — The farmer with the APIA problem (our #1 ranking, wasted)

**Today.** A man farming near Ocna Mureș is told by APIA that his parcel is not in order. He searches **`intabulare teren agricol alba`** on his phone. Our site comes up **first — position #1**, above every directory in the county. He taps it, and lands on a page titled *"Intabulare teren în **Cut**"* — a village of about a thousand people, 55 km from Aiud, that has nothing to do with him. Under the headline in Google he had already read *"aprox. 55 km de Aiud"*. He goes back and taps the next result.

**We are winning the search and losing the customer at the door.**

**After.** The same search lands him on **"Intabulare teren agricol în județul Alba"** — a county page listing every locality we cover with the distance, which BCPI office his file goes to, what the file costs, and what to send us. He taps the green button and the message is already written: *"Bună ziua! Am un teren agricol în județul Alba și am nevoie de intabulare — vă trimit o poză a actului."* He attaches a photo of his titlu de proprietate and sends it.

---

### Flow 2 — Ana, selling an apartment in Alba Iulia, three weeks to the notary

**Today.** She searches **`intabulare apartament Alba Iulia`** at 9 in the evening. There is **no map with pins** on this search — it is pure organic, so our office being in Aiud costs us nothing. We appear at **#4**. She taps, and the page is about **intabularea unei case / construcții** — not an apartment. She is not sure it applies to her, so she goes back to necesit.ro.

**After.** The same search shows *"Intabulare apartament în Alba Iulia — Topograf autorizat"*, and the snippet says what she cares about: acte, pași, prețuri orientative, and which office the file goes to. The page opens not with a slogan but with her situation: *"Vindeți un apartament în Alba Iulia și notarul v-a cerut intabularea?"* One bold line tells her exactly what to send. She taps the green button: *"Bună ziua! Am nevoie de intabulare pentru un apartament în Alba Iulia. Am termen de la notar."* She sends it without editing a word, at 9 pm, from the sofa.

---

### Flow 3 — Ion, 58, inherited land near Aiud

**Today.** He types **`topograf aiud`**, without diacritics. He sees the map first: **our business is #1 in that map pack** — Fleser Aurel, Str. Florilor 1A, 5.0 stars. Then, below it, our homepage at **#4**. This flow already works. Two things weaken it: the profile shows only **3 reviews** while the competitor next door shows 5, and if he searches `cadastru si intabulare Aiud` instead, Google shows him our **land** page rather than our **cadastru și intabulare** page — close enough that he probably does not notice, but it is the same defect.

**After.** The map result carries 15 reviews instead of 3. The page names the man who will come to the measurement — *Aurel Fleser, inginer topograf autorizat ANCPI* — with his authorization number, and tells Ion the simplest possible action: *"Faceți o poză actului de proprietate și trimiteți-o pe WhatsApp — mesajul este deja scris."* If he would rather call, the number on the page is dialable and always has been.

---

### Flow 4 — The builder in Alba Iulia who needs a plan topografic

**Today.** He searches **`plan topografic Alba Iulia`**. We rank **#2** — the second result in the county capital, on a deadline-driven query, two days after launch. Google serves him *"Plan topografic în **Doștat**"*, a commune 53 km away. He is standing on a site in Alba Iulia. He leaves.

**After.** He gets the Alba Iulia page, which states the office, the process, and the price, and he messages on WhatsApp with the address of his site. This is the one segment that comes back for every future project.

---

### Flow 5 — Splitting land before a sale (dezmembrare)

**Today.** Someone dividing a plot searches **`dezmembrare teren Alba`**. We rank **#2**, and Google serves **Berghin** — 35 km from Aiud, and not where the searcher is. At #6 we appear again, this time as **Teiuș**. Two of our own pages competing on one search, neither of them the right one.

**After.** The county query lands on *"Dezmembrare și alipire teren în județul Alba"*, which explains what the notary will need, what it costs per resulting lot, and links down to the specific town. Dezmembrare is, per Team A, one of the highest-value jobs on the board — this is the most expensive wrong page on the site.

---

### Flow 6 — The one search we genuinely lose, stated honestly

**`cadastru si intabulare Alba Iulia`** — the head term for the county capital. We are **not in the top 10**. The map pack is six Alba Iulia firms, several of them clustered on the street beside the OCPI office; proximity is the deciding factor and our office is 30 km away. Six of the ten organic results are directories. **This is not winnable with page edits in this cycle, and we are not going to spend a flagship pretending otherwise.** The service-specific Alba Iulia searches (Flows 2 and 4) have no map pack at all — that is where the same effort pays.

## 5. What this does NOT establish

- **SERP observation is correlational.** That Google serves Cut for a county query is a measured fact. That building a county hub will change it is a **hypothesis** — well-grounded in how hierarchy and internal linking generally work, unproven here. It is confirmed or refuted by one thing: the **Search Console "Pages" report at 2026-08-19** showing which URL Google serves per query. If the served URL does not change, the hypothesis failed and we say so.
- **Two-day-old rankings are unstable.** New sites often get a temporary boost that decays. #1 on `intabulare teren agricol alba` today may be lower on 19 August **regardless of anything we do**. A drop is not automatically our fault, and a hold is not automatically our success.
- **Conversion changes are not measurable with what we have.** GSC counts clicks, not WhatsApp messages. The "what to send" block is judged on reasoning, not evidence, unless Dan starts noting where enquiries come from.
- **Nobody can promise a #1.** Not from this data, not from this method.

**Measurement gate — 2026-08-19 (three weeks post-launch), record all four:**
1. Position and impressions for the ten captured queries.
2. **Which URL Google serves for each** — the cannibalization metric.
3. Index coverage: how many of the 2,095 pages are indexed vs "Crawled – currently not indexed". If the indexed share is small, the answer is fewer and stronger pages, not more.
4. Map-pack position in Aiud and Ocna Mureș, against the review count on that date.
