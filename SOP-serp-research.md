# SOP — SERP research and page-competitiveness process

**Version 1.0 · 2026-07-29 · Binding on every research and implementation agent.**

Goal: every page on expert-intabulare.ro competes for the search that page is for, and beats
whatever currently holds position 1 for that intent.

Read this in full before doing anything. Then follow it literally.

## 1. The research unit — read this before proposing to research "every page"

The site has **2,095 pages**. They are NOT 2,095 distinct search intents.

| Layer | Count | Does intent vary? |
|---|---|---|
| Service | 12 | **YES** — `dezmembrare` vs `certificat energetic` are different searches, different competitors, different buyer |
| Locality tier | 3 (municipiu / oraș / comună) | **YES** — cities have map packs and local rivals; villages return near-empty SERPs dominated by national directories |
| County | 5 | **PARTLY** — local competitor sets differ; national directories are constant |
| Individual locality | ~170 | **NO** — `intabulare teren Aiud` and `intabulare teren Teiuș` differ only by place name |

**Therefore the research unit is `service × tier`, sampled across counties — roughly 50–70 SERP
captures, not 8,000.** Findings are applied to all localities through the generator, which is what
makes every page benefit.

Researching each locality separately would issue ~8,000 Google queries (Google will block it long
before completion) and produce ~170 near-identical reports per service. **Do not do it.** If you
believe a specific locality genuinely has a distinct SERP, capture that one as an exception and
say why.

## 2. Target list construction

For each of the 12 services (`cadastru-si-intabulare`, `intabulare-teren`,
`intabulare-casa-constructie`, `intabulare-apartament`, `dezmembrare-alipire`, `plan-topografic`,
`trasare-teren`, `actualizare-carte-funciara`, `topografie-constructii`, `servicii-ocpi`,
`certificat-energetic`, `expertize-documentatii`) build **3–5 queries**:

- **Q1 — county head:** `<service> alba` (e.g. `dezmembrare teren alba`)
- **Q2 — city:** `<service> alba iulia`
- **Q3 — home town:** `<service> aiud`
- **Q4 — second city:** `<service> sebes` OR `<service> ocna mures` OR `<service> blaj` (rotate)
- **Q5 — colloquial variant:** how a real person types it, no diacritics, possibly fragmentary
  (`topograf aiud`, `cadastru apartament pret`, `acte dezmembrare teren`)

Write queries **as a Romanian types them: no diacritics, lowercase.**

Record each as:
```json
{ "id": "", "query": "", "service": "", "tier": "county|city|town", "locality": null,
  "intent": "transactional|informational", "source": "seed|autocomplete|related" }
```
`tier:"county"` uses `locality: null`. Only `intent:"transactional"` proceeds past step 4.

## 3. STEP-BY-STEP — follow exactly, per query

### Step 1 — Capture the SERP
Navigate to:
```
https://www.google.com/search?q=<url-encoded>&hl=ro&gl=ro&num=10
```
Extract with `get_page_text`. Record **separately** — never merge:
- **Map pack** ("Rezultate locale"): each business name, rating, review count, category, address.
- **Organic top 10**: rank, URL, domain, title, snippet.
- **Related searches** ("Alții caută și").
- **PAA** if present. *(Observed: 0 of 10 queries returned one. Expect empty.)*
- **Our position**: rank AND — critically — **which of our URLs Google served.**

> **Map pack and organic are different ranking systems.** Map pack is driven by Google Business
> Profile signals (category, proximity, review count). **Page content cannot move it.** Never
> propose a page change to fix a map-pack position.

If a CAPTCHA appears: STOP, tell the operator, wait. Do not retry more than twice.

### Step 2 — Classify every organic result by `pageType`
**Mandatory. Do not skip.**

`surveyor` | `directory` | `marketplace` | `blog` | `government` | `other`

Known directories in this vertical: necesit.ro, intabulari24.ro, firmecadastru.ro,
birou-cadastru.com, birouri-cadastru.ro, cylex.ro, olx.ro, bizoo.ro, termene.ro, topfirme.com,
cadastru24.ro, efunciara.ro, cfunciara.ro, cf.ro, extrase.ro, brig.ro, daibau.ro, expert24.ro,
sicap.ai, listafirme.ro, deschis.ro, paginiaurii.ro.

**Roughly 6 of 10 results are directories.** Computing "what the top results do" over directory
listings produces meaningless targets — a "Top 20 firme" listicle is not a page we can or should
imitate. **All consensus is computed over the `surveyor` cohort ONLY.** Record the aggregator
share per query separately; a high share means the query is winnable with a genuine service page.

### Step 3 — Open and read the top 3–5 SURVEYOR results
Skip directories. If fewer than 3 surveyor results exist in the top 10, take what exists and
record the shortfall — do not pad with directories.

Prefer `WebFetch` (parallelizable, no browser needed). Use Chrome only if the page is
JS-rendered and WebFetch returns nothing useful.

Extract into this exact schema:

```json
{
  "url": "", "domain": "", "pageType": "surveyor",
  "rankedFor": [{"queryId": "", "rank": 0}],
  "wordCount": 0,
  "headingOutline": [{"level": 1, "text": ""}],
  "sectionsPresent": [],
  "questionsAnswered": [],
  "priceDisclosure": "none|range|exact",
  "priceValues": "",
  "durationClaim": "",
  "acteNecesare": {"present": false, "itemCount": 0},
  "processSteps": {"present": false, "stepCount": 0},
  "faq": {"present": false, "questionCount": 0},
  "schemaTypes": [],
  "trustSignals": {"ancpiAuthNumber": false, "namedPerson": false, "photoOfPerson": false,
                   "reviews": false, "yearsExperience": false, "portfolio": false,
                   "phoneVisible": false, "whatsapp": false},
  "ctaStyle": "",
  "localSpecificity": "none|generic|named-places|deep",
  "whatTheyDoBetter": [],
  "whatWeDoBetter": []
}
```

Record `durationClaim` and `priceValues` as **intelligence about competitors**. We do not publish
ours (ruling 0.2) — we record theirs to know what the customer has already been told.

### Step 4 — Score OUR page on the identical schema
Find our page: `docs/servicii/<service>/<county>/<locality>.html` — **flat `.html` leaf, not
`<locality>/index.html`.** For `tier:"county"`, our page is `docs/zone/<county>/index.html`.

**Score the page Google ACTUALLY SERVES, and separately the page it SHOULD serve.** If they
differ, that is a routing defect — record it as `servedPage` vs `correctPage` and flag it. This
is currently the site's single biggest problem.

### Step 5 — Gap analysis, confidence-tagged
Per query, compare our page to the **surveyor-cohort** consensus.

- **HIGH** — near-unanimous among surveyor results AND mechanically checkable
  (e.g. "4 of 4 surveyors name the individual surveyor; we do not").
- **MEDIUM** — majority pattern with a plausible mechanism.
- **LOW** — correlational only (e.g. word count). Length correlates with rank without causing it.

Every gap states: **moves rankings** or **table stakes** (needed for credibility, unlikely to
move position). Gaps that would require violating ruling 0.2 are recorded as **BLOCKED BY POLICY**
and NOT actioned.

### Step 6 — Output a CONTENT SPEC, not prose
Research agents do **not** write Romanian page copy. Output a spec the implementation stage
executes:

```markdown
## <service> — <tier>
- Target queries: ...
- Our current position / page served / page that should be served:
- Surveyor cohort analysed (N): ...
- Aggregator share: N/10
- GAPS (ranked, confidence-tagged):
  1. [HIGH][moves-rankings] ...
- CONTENT SPEC — what the page must contain:
  - Section: <name> — purpose, what it must answer, data source
  - ...
- DATA REQUIRED FROM GENERATOR: which fields exist / which are missing
- BLOCKED BY POLICY: ...
- ROUTING DEFECT: yes/no + detail
```

## 4. Implementation stage — separate agents, strict rules

1. **Single writer per file.** Multiple agents must never edit one generator file. Territories are
   assigned; if you need something outside yours, STOP and escalate.
2. All content lands in `generator/data/**` or `generator/lib/**`. **Never `docs/`.**
3. **Gate after EVERY change, no exceptions:**
   ```
   node generator/build.js && node generator/verify.js && node research/seo-2026-07/check-demotions.js
   ```
   All three must pass. The demotion guard compares **key sets**, not counts — baseline is
   currently **0 demotions**, its strictest setting. Any newly demoted page means you deleted a
   live page: revert and rewrite.
4. Rebuild after each logical change, not once per batch, so regressions stay attributable.
5. `git commit` with **exact pathspecs**. **Never `git add .`** — agents share one tree.
6. **Do not push.** The operator decides.
7. Every added passage gets a 3-word shingle check against all existing `localNote` values and the
   `pages.js` frame pools. No run of two or more consecutive shared shingles.
8. **Do not resize `faqPool` or `introVariants`.** They sit at ~45% document frequency, just under
   the 50% boilerplate cut; resizing is a service-wide overlap cliff.

## 5. Gate mechanics you must understand before writing content

From `generator/lib/gates.js`, verified:
- `MIN_UNIQUE_WORDS = 120` page-exclusive words; `MAX_SHINGLE_OVERLAP = 0.7` pairwise;
  `MAX_LEAF_BYTES = 61440`.
- Sibling groups are **per SERVICE across ALL FIVE COUNTIES** (`build.js:208`), ~222 pages.
- Shingles in **≥50% of the group are dropped as boilerplate** before overlap is measured.
  - Text identical across all ~222 pages → boilerplate → does NOT drive overlap. **Safe.**
  - Text constant per **county** (~35%), per **BCPI office** (5–11%), or per **profile** → lands
    in the residual → **DOES drive overlap.** Dangerous as a shared leaf block.
  - Genuinely per-locality text → page-exclusive → **raises unique words. Ideal.**
- **Rescues perturb the whole group both ways.** Adding pages moves the boilerplate cut; pages
  near the floor can be pushed under by another page's success. Observed twice this session on
  pages nobody edited. **Never trust the demotion count — only the key set.**

## 6. Anti-patterns — these fail review

- Computing competitor consensus over directory listings.
- Proposing a page change to fix a map-pack position.
- Publishing the exact ANCPI tariff, or ANY turnaround/duration claim about BCPI processing
  (ruling 0.2). **Note: existing hedged orientative price RANGES are allowed and live — proposing
  to remove them is itself an anti-pattern.**
- Inventing PNCCF years, sector numbers, parcel counts, or dates.
- Per-locality text that only swaps the place name — precisely what the overlap gate catches.
- Hand-editing `docs/`.
- Claiming a fix will produce a #1 ranking. SERP analysis is correlational.
- Researching all ~170 localities of a service separately (see §1).

---

## 7. What this method cannot establish

SERP analysis shows **correlation, not causation**. That the top result has a price table does not
establish the table causes the ranking. Domain age and authority are large factors no on-page work
touches.

All findings ship as **ranked hypotheses with explicit confidence**, never as a ranking recipe.

**Measurement gate — 2026-08-19 (Google Search Console):** position and impressions per query;
**which URL Google serves per query** (the routing metric); index coverage (indexed vs
"Crawled – currently not indexed"); map-pack position vs review count. Nothing is promoted
site-wide on the strength of this research alone.
