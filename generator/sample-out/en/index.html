Who we are

Fleser Aurel Expert is an ANCPI-licensed land surveying practice based in Aiud, Alba county — the heart of Transylvania. Over 25 years we have completed more than 1,052 projects: cadastral registrations, topographic surveys, boundary stakeouts and land due diligence for private owners, developers and investors.

How we work with international clients

WhatsApp is our default channel: describe your project, attach the land-book extract, and you will get a clear answer about feasibility, timeline and cost. Documents can be signed remotely through a proxy — many of our clients live abroad.
