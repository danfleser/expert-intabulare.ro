## Headline findings

### F1 — The site ALREADY RANKS on home-town queries (contradicts the "too new to rank" prior)

The domain went live 2026-07-28. Two days later:

| Query | Organic rank | Page Google chose |
|---|---|---|
| `topograf aiud` | **#4** | homepage |
| `cadastru si intabulare Aiud` | **#5** | `/servicii/intabulare-teren/alba/aiud` |
| `intabulare apartament Alba Iulia` | **#4** | intabulare **casă/construcție** Alba Iulia |
| `intabulare teren Alba Iulia` | **#9** | intabulare teren **Ohaba** |
| `cadastru si intabulare Alba Iulia` | not in top 10 | — |

MAIN's earlier statement that a two-day-old domain "cannot rank" was **wrong for the local long
tail**. It remains true for county/city head terms (see F4).

### F2 — WRONG-PAGE SELECTION (keyword cannibalization). Highest-priority defect found.

On the two queries where an exact-match page exists, Google picked a **sibling instead**:

- `intabulare teren Alba Iulia` → served **Ohaba** (a village ~37 km away), not alba-iulia
- `intabulare apartament Alba Iulia` → served **intabulare-casa-constructie**, not intabulare-apartament

Both correct pages exist and are emitted. This is the classic doorway-cluster failure: 927
near-identical service×locality pages give Google insufficient signal to disambiguate, so it
picks semi-arbitrarily.

**No content improvement fixes this.** It is an internal-linking / canonical / differentiation
problem, and it is upstream of everything in the current plan. A flagship page rewritten to
perfection still loses if Google serves a different page for the query.

## Note on PAA

**Not one of the five queries returned a People Also Ask block.** The plan's Task 3 schema treats
`paa` as a standard field; for this vertical and geo it is consistently empty. Related searches
are the substitute signal, and they skew heavily navigational (`ocpi alba`, `ocpi alba contact`,
`cadastru alba iulia program` — people looking for the government office and its opening hours,
not for a surveyor).
