- **ROUTING DEFECT: YES — and this is the cleanest proof of the cannibalization thesis captured so
  far.** In every earlier instance (Cut, Doștat, Ohaba, Berghin, Șibot) only the wrong page was

  the same SERP simultaneously, and Google has ranked the village above the city by seven
  positions.** The two pages are directly compared by Google on one query and the near-empty
  commune wins. That is not a proximity effect, not a query-interpretation effect, and not a
  missing page — it is the two pages being indistinguishable and the ordering falling out
  arbitrarily.
- The #9 snippet again carries the meta template with the distance clause:
  "…acte, pași, prețuri orientative. Topograf autorizat ANCPI, **aprox. 39 km de Aiud**."

### Related searches
**intabulare casa alba bucurești** · **intabulare casa alba cluj-napoca**

**Read this carefully — it is a query-interpretation warning.** Google's related block for
`intabulare casa alba` offers *Bucharest* and *Cluj-Napoca* variants, i.e. it partly parses
"casa alba" as a **noun phrase ("the white house")** rather than "casă" + county "Alba". The
county token is weak on this query and the entity is ambiguous. Any W1 hub for this service
should lean on **"județul Alba"** and named Alba localities, not the bare token "alba".

### OUR position — **#8. WRONG PAGE — wrong locality AND wrong service.**
- **servedPage:** `docs/servicii/cadastru-si-intabulare/alba/noslac.html` — Noșlac, a **different
  commune**, served for an **Aiud** query.

### A mechanism, visible in the snippet — this is the most useful line in the batch
Google chose this sentence from the Noșlac page as the snippet:

> "Pentru imobilele din Noșlac, documentația cadastrală se depune la **BCPI Aiud, Str. Cuza Vodă
> nr. 14, Aiud.** Ne ocupăm noi de depunere și de urmărirea dosarului."

**Google matched the query token "aiud" against our BCPI-office block, not against the page's
locality.** The office block names Aiud three times on every page in the BCPI Aiud catchment —
Noșlac, Rădești, Lopadea Nouă, Livezile, Mirăslău, Aiud itself and the rest. Every one of those
pages therefore reads to Google as an "Aiud" page, and the actual Aiud leaf has no distinguishing
signal against ~15 siblings all shouting "Aiud".

Note the same Noșlac page was served for the county query `cadastru si intabulare alba` (#7). One
commune page is now confirmed to be absorbing **two** unrelated queries via office-name matching.

**This is independent, mechanistic support for chair ruling D4** (office-constant text belongs on
hubs and flagships, never as a shared block on leaves). Team C's argument for D4 was about the
overlap gate; **this is the same block causing a second, separate harm in the SERP itself.** It
should be recorded as a new argument for D4, not a repeat of the old one.

**Caveat, stated per SOP §7:** that Google chose this snippet establishes the *match*, not the
*ranking cause*. Removing office text from leaves is a hypothesis for the 2026-08-19 gate, and
office routing is genuinely useful information for the customer — the remedy is to make the Aiud
leaf unambiguously stronger on "Aiud", not necessarily to strip the office block from siblings.
