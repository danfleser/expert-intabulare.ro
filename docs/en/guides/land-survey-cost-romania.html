How pricing works — two layers, no mystery

Every cadastral or surveying job in Romania has two cost layers, and understanding them protects you from both overpaying and unrealistic cheap quotes:

  • Official fees — ANCPI/OCPI tariffs for registrations, extracts and receptions. These are fixed by law, identical whoever you hire, and most have a legal urgency option that multiplies the fee in exchange for much faster processing.
  • The surveyor's fee — the market-priced work: measurement, documentation, filing and follow-up. This is where quotes differ, and where the differences usually reflect scope: does the quote include responding to OCPI completion requests? Boundary agreement with neighbours? Delivery of design-ready files?

We publish indicative ranges, never fixed prices — because terrain, documentation history and legal complications genuinely change the work. Anyone quoting a firm price for a parcel they have never seen documents for is guessing.

What moves the price up — and what we tell you upfront

The honest cost drivers: unresolved inheritance (the succession must be settled before registration), discrepancies between deed area and measured area, overlaps with neighbouring parcels, old paper land books needing conversion, difficult terrain or access, and urgency. None of these are surcharges we invent — they are extra work, and we itemize them in the quote so you can see exactly why a complicated parcel costs more than a clean one.

For foreign clients, add the notary's and translator's fees on the legal side (power of attorney, deeds) — not our services, but we tell you to budget for them so the total cost of ownership is never a surprise.

Întrebări frecvente

Why do quotes from different surveyors differ so much?

Mostly scope. A low quote often covers measurement and filing but excludes follow-up: OCPI completion requests, neighbour signatures, corrections. Compare what is included, not just the number — the cheapest quote that stalls at the registrar's first objection is the most expensive one.

Are the official ANCPI fees included in your quotes?

We list them separately and transparently — they are legally fixed pass-through amounts, and separating them shows you exactly what our work costs versus what the state charges.

Is the urgency fee worth paying?

When a transaction, grant deadline or financing milestone depends on the registration date — almost always yes. The urgency tariff is often small relative to what a missed deadline costs. We calculate both paths so you can decide.

Do you charge for travel to the site?

Within Alba county, travel is included in our fees. In the neighbouring counties it is calculated by distance and stated as its own line in the quote — visible before you commit, never discovered on the invoice.
