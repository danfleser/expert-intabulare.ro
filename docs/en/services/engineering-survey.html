The surveyor as part of your project team

Beyond one-off plans and registrations, construction projects need surveying as a continuous discipline: reliable terrain data before design, geometric control during execution, and documented evidence at the end. That is engineering surveying (topografie inginerească), and it is where 25+ years of field practice matter most.

We work as the surveying arm of design offices, urbanists, contractors and developers on projects across Alba, Cluj, Mureș, Sibiu and Hunedoara counties — from single industrial halls to multi-stage developments and PV plants.

What this covers

  • Surveys for zoning documentation (PUZ/PUD): Romanian zoning plans are drawn on an OCPI-received topographic base — we produce it and steer it through the office's reception procedure, a step with its own rules and timelines that regularly surprises foreign developers.
  • Design-stage terrain data: topographic surveys, digital terrain models, longitudinal and cross profiles for roads and utility routes, so engineers design on measured reality.
  • Volume and earthworks calculations: cut/fill computations for platforms and grading — often the difference between a profitable and an unprofitable site.
  • Execution control: stakeout campaigns, level checks on foundations and structures, verification measurements at handover points between contractors.
  • Precise leveling and settlement monitoring: installing benchmarks and running measurement cycles on structures where movement must be tracked over time, with reports your insurer or lender can use.
  • As-built documentation: final measurements proving what was actually built, for acceptance, registration and operation.

How we work with international project teams

Deliverables come in the formats your team already uses — DWG/DXF, coordinate lists, PDF reports, terrain models — with drawings and correspondence in English where you need them. We quote per deliverable or as a project-long support arrangement with agreed mobilization times. On multi-contractor sites, an independent surveyor giving all parties the same geometric truth prevents the classic dispute where each contractor measures in their own favour.

What we do not do, and say so plainly: geotechnical studies, structural design, or environmental permitting. Those belong to other licensed professions; we coordinate with the specialists who hold them, our measurements feed their work, and your project gets one coherent geometric dataset instead of three competing ones.

Întrebări frecvente

Can you support a project through its whole lifecycle?

Yes — that is the normal pattern: survey for design, stakeout at works start, control measurements during execution, as-built at the end. One surveyor holding the site's geometry from start to finish removes an entire class of coordination errors.

Do you work as a subcontractor to design offices or contractors?

We collaborate with developers, designers and construction companies on exactly that basis, with deliverables specified per their project standards.

What equipment do you use?

Survey-grade GNSS/RTK receivers using the national ROMPOS correction network, a total station for high-accuracy and obstructed work, optical leveling for precise height work, and a survey drone for large-area mapping.

How is project-long surveying support priced?

Either per deliverable against a rate card agreed at the start, or as a retainer-style arrangement with committed mobilization times for projects that need us on call. For a multi-month build, agreeing the structure upfront is cheaper for you and lets us actually reserve the capacity — ad-hoc callouts are always the expensive way to buy surveying.

Our project is stuck at the OCPI reception of the PUZ survey. Can you take over?

Often, yes. Completion requests from the office usually name concrete, fixable defects — wrong coordinate framing, missing pieces, outdated parcel references. We diagnose the file, tell you plainly whether it is repairable or needs redoing, and carry it through the procedure. Inheriting half-done documentation is not our favourite work, but it is work we know how to finish.
