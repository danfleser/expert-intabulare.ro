An honest coverage statement

We are a local practice, and we believe that is a feature: surveying quality depends on knowing the terrain, the county OCPI's habits, and the town halls — and on being able to be on your site quickly, repeatedly, without cross-country mobilization costs. So instead of claiming all of Romania, here is exactly what we cover.

Field work — measurements, cadastral documentation, stakeout, drone flights, as-built surveys — covers Alba county (home base: Aiud) and the four neighbouring counties: Cluj, Mureș, Sibiu and Hunedoara. That is a working radius spanning most of central Transylvania, including the Alba Iulia–Aiud–Turda corridor, the Cluj basin, the Târnave valleys and the Mureș and Sebeș valleys — an area with dense solar, industrial and agricultural activity.

Desk services — land book extracts and verification, due diligence on titles and documentation, decoding OCPI correspondence, coordinating with notaries — cover property anywhere in Romania, because they run on the national electronic registers, not on travel.

For larger projects

For utility-scale solar sites and multi-phase projects within the five counties, we commit to agreed mobilization terms across the project's life — the practical advantage of a surveyor 30–90 minutes from site rather than a day away. For substantial projects at the edges of this range, ask: the honest answer depends on scope, and where we are not the right choice logistically, we say so rather than delivering slow service. Travel within Alba county is included in our fees; in neighbouring counties it is calculated by distance and stated in the quote — never a surprise line item.

Not sure which side of the line your site falls on? Send the location (a pin, a locality name, or a cadastral number) on WhatsApp and you will have your answer quickly — along with whether we are the right surveyor for the job at all.

Întrebări frecvente

Our portfolio has sites both inside and outside your field range. How do we work?

We take the full portfolio's desk work — verification and due diligence are location-independent — and the field work within the five counties. For the rest we tell you plainly it is outside our range; no site is served badly for the sake of taking everything.

Why a five-county limit when other firms advertise national coverage?

Because field surveying done well is local: repeat visits, county-office familiarity, and same-week availability all degrade with distance. Our range is the one within which we can promise the service level our 25 years and 1,052+ projects were built on.

Does the coverage limit apply to OCPI paperwork too?

Filing and following cadastral files is tied to where the property lies, since each county's OCPI handles its own territory — but the electronic system means we routinely manage files at any of the five counties' offices from Aiud, and pure register lookups work nationally. What needs physical presence is the measuring, which is exactly what the five-county range is drawn around.

Can you recommend someone for a site outside your range?

Where we know a trustworthy authorized colleague in the region, we say so — the profession is small and reputations are known. What we will not do is quietly subcontract your site to an unknown and put our name on it.
