Why so much Romanian land is not in the land book

A large share of Romanian rural land — the very extravilan agricultural land solar projects want — has never been through first registration. The historical reason: post-1991 restitution returned land through property titles (titluri de proprietate) that identified parcels by agricultural grid references (tarla and parcelă numbers), not by surveyed coordinates. Owners hold valid paper titles, but the parcels have no cadastral number, no land book, and often no measured boundaries. Add three decades of unsettled inheritances, and a 40-hectare target site can mean dozens of owners, some deceased, some abroad, some unknown.

None of this makes a site impossible. It makes it a sequencing problem with a knowable timeline — and the developers who treat it that way from day one are the ones whose projects keep their schedules.

The paths to a registered parcel

  • Standard first registration (sporadic cadastre): we measure the parcel, prepare the documentation from the property title and supporting acts, and file with OCPI. For a clean single-owner parcel, realistic total time is weeks — commonly 3–8 from measurement to land book, depending on office workload and urgency fees.
  • Inheritance first: where the titled owner is deceased, the notarial succession must be settled before or alongside registration — this, not the cadastral work, is usually the schedule driver. We coordinate with the notary so the cadastral file is ready the day the succession closes.
  • Parcel identification: where owners hold titles but nobody is sure which land is whose, the parcelling plan (plan parcelar) of the tarla and archival research establish defensible identification before anything is filed.
  • Systematic registration: some communes are covered by the state's free, area-wide registration program. Where it is genuinely imminent it can help; where it is merely announced, waiting for it is how projects die. We check the actual local status.

Multi-owner strategy: the order of operations

For a site of many unregistered parcels, the sequence that works: identify and verify every parcel and owner (desk due diligence + parcelling plans) → secure agreements with owners while registrations proceed in parallel → first-register parcel by parcel → consolidate via merging and shape the site via division → register the project's rights (purchase or superficie). Running registrations in parallel batches rather than serially is the single biggest schedule lever — and pricing the registration timeline into the option agreements is the single biggest commercial one.

We run this machinery locally: measurement crews on site, files at the right OCPI office, notaries and town halls that answer. That is what "local surveyor" means for a project's critical path.

Întrebări frecvente

Can we sign purchase agreements on unregistered land?

Pre-agreements, yes — final notarial transfer requires the land book. The standard structure is a pre-sale agreement conditioned on registration, with the registration work (ours) running against a defined timeline. Your lawyer drafts; we deliver the registrations that let closings happen.

How do you price first registration across many parcels?

Per parcel, with volume structure for batches — and we scope honestly after seeing the titles, because a parcel with a settled succession and one with three unsettled inheritances are different products.

What is the realistic worst case?

A parcel with contested inheritance or litigation can take many months and is fundamentally a legal problem. Our job is to identify those parcels in due diligence so you can route the layout around them — or price them accordingly — instead of discovering them at closing.

Who typically hires you on these projects — the developer or the landowners?

Usually the developer, who funds the registrations as part of the land cost; occasionally prepared sellers register first to command a better price. Either way the work is identical, and a developer-funded registration program with owner cooperation clauses in the pre-agreements is the structure that moves fastest.
